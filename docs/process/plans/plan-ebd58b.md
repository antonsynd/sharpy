## Design Decisions

2. **`EnterStore` records a `StoreContext`.** A small record `StoreContext(StorePosition Position, SemanticType Slot, string? CalleeDisplay, int? ArgumentOrdinal, string? KeywordName)` is saved/restored by `StoreScope` in a new field `_storeContext`. 3. **Constructors infer at every expectation.** `TryCheckTaggedUnionConstructor`: `Some(v)` under a NON-Optional expectation (and no user-defined `Some`) types as `Optional[typeof v]` (argument checked with no expectation) and returns it — the enclosing seam then refuses it into `int | None`/`int` with SPY0220 and `DescribeStoreRefusalSteer`'s unwrap steer (exactly what `o: int? = Some(5); y: int | None = o` reports today). `None()` under a non-Optional expectation keeps SPY0244 (the established Row-1 contract of plan-757fbb; `StoreTargetMatrixTests` asserts it). `Ok(v)`/`Err(e)` under a non-Result expectation: the seam's SPY0220 at the call span, value displayed as `Result[int32, _]` / `Result[_, str]`, steer "declare the slot `T!E` or match on the Result" — never SPY0230. `CheckIdentifier`'s bare-constructor arm stays for a genuinely uncalled `x = Some`.
